About

I am a China Scholarship Council joint PhD student in the MRC Centre for Environment and Health at Imperial College London, working with Prof Paolo Vineis and Dr Oliver Robinson, and a PhD candidate at Jiangnan University with Prof Xueming Xu.

At Imperial I work on three projects: an inflammation protein score for healthspan, built in UK Biobank and carried into EPIC, REACT and Airwave; a comparison of that score with proteomic ageing clocks; and the health effects of hot weather. Before that I spent several years at the bench, in mouse models of obesity and colitis with lipidomics, proteomics, metagenomics and RNA-seq, so I read population signals with the biology in mind.

Research

At Imperial College London three projects · two papers in preparation

Paper 1 · in preparation

One inflammation score, four cohorts

Can a blood test for inflammation tell us who will lose healthy years first? From 2,920 Olink proteins in 36,147 UK Biobank participants, I built a 69-protein score for the first of seven major chronic diseases or death. It adds information beyond age, including within narrow age groups. I then applied it with unchanged weights in EPIC (SomaScan), REACT and Airwave, each inside a trusted research environment.

LASSO-Cox · cross-validation · bootstrap · calibration · external validation

Paper 2 · in preparation

Inflammation and proteomic ageing clocks

Proteomic clocks estimate how fast a person is ageing. Is inflammation the part of that signal that matters for health? In the same people and the same proteins, I compare a targeted inflammation score with proteomic ageing clocks for predicting healthspan.

Head-to-head prediction · inflammaging · biological age

Grant application

Heat and cardiometabolic health

As summers get hotter, what happens to blood pressure and the kidney? I linked outdoor temperature to blood pressure in about 470,000 UK Biobank participants and used a case-crossover design for hospital admissions after hot days. The work supports an Imperial-led application to the Novo Nordisk Foundation on heat and hypertension, for which I run the cohort analyses.

Exposure linkage · case-crossover · UK Biobank, REACT, Airwave

Clinical Nutrition 2025

Diet, obesity and the gut–brain axis

In a four-month high-fat-diet mouse model I combined serum lipidomics, hippocampal proteomics, cytokines, histology and memory tests. Lycopene lowered pro-inflammatory lipids and protected memory through taurine and glutathione pathways. A second study on torularhodin is in revision.

Read the paper →

Microbiology Spectrum 2022

A probiotic against colitis

Two colitis mouse trials, one head to head against the drug sulfasalazine, showed that Lactobacillus plantarum HNU082 eased disease by restoring the gut barrier and reshaping the microbiome. The paper has been cited more than 270 times, and the strain is patented and supplied to industry.

Read the paper → · Food & Function 2022 →

Food Research International 2025

Fermentation and evidence grading

A co-fermented sourdough that keeps bread fresh without chemical preservatives, and three first-author critical reviews. The two most recent grade every mechanistic claim on a predefined five-level evidence scale.
