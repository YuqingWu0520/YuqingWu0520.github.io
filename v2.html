About

I am a China Scholarship Council joint PhD student in the MRC Centre for Environment and Health at Imperial College London, working with Prof Paolo Vineis and Dr Oliver Robinson, and a PhD candidate at Jiangnan University with Prof Xueming Xu.

My work at Imperial comprises three projects: a plasma inflammation score for healthspan, derived in UK Biobank and applied in EPIC, REACT and Airwave; its comparison with proteomic ageing clocks; and the cardiometabolic effects of ambient heat. It builds on several years of experimental research in mouse models of obesity and colitis, integrating lipidomics, proteomics, metagenomics and transcriptomics, which allows me to interpret population-scale protein signals in mechanistic terms.

I have published nine peer-reviewed papers, eight as first or joint first author, and hold two invention patents, one of them licensed to industry. My doctoral training is supported by a China Scholarship Council scholarship, and I was selected for the China Association for Science and Technology Young Talent Support Programme. I review for Microbiology Spectrum, Food & Function and Clinical Nutrition, and our probiotic research has been featured by China Daily and other media.

Research

At Imperial College London three projects · two papers in preparation

Paper 1 · in preparation

One inflammation score, four cohorts

Can a blood test for inflammation identify who will lose healthy years first? From 2,920 Olink proteins measured in 36,147 UK Biobank participants, I derived a 69-protein inflammation score that predicts the first of seven major chronic diseases, or death. The score adds prognostic information beyond chronological age, including within narrow age strata, and was applied with fixed weights in EPIC (SomaScan), REACT and Airwave within trusted research environments.

LASSO-Cox · cross-validation · bootstrap · calibration · external validation

Paper 2 · in preparation

Inflammation and proteomic ageing clocks

Proteomic clocks estimate the pace of biological ageing. Is inflammation the component of that signal that matters most for health? Within the same individuals and protein measurements, I compare a targeted inflammation score with proteomic ageing clocks as predictors of healthspan.

Head-to-head prediction · inflammaging · biological age

Grant application

Heat and cardiometabolic health

How does rising ambient temperature affect blood pressure and kidney health? I linked outdoor temperature to blood pressure in approximately 470,000 UK Biobank participants and used a case-crossover design to examine hospital admissions following hot days. This work underpins an Imperial-led application to the Novo Nordisk Foundation on heat and hypertension, for which I conduct the cohort analyses.

Exposure linkage · case-crossover · UK Biobank, REACT, Airwave

Probiotics: from fermented food to a licensed strain Hainan University · 2020–2023 · with Prof Jiachao Zhang

One strain, five steps: how Lactiplantibacillus plantarum HNU082 went from a field survey of fermented foods to a patent licensed to industry. I ran the animal and multi-omic studies and am joint first author of the two main papers.

  1. 1 · DiscoverSurvey and isolate

    Shotgun metagenomics and 16S sequencing of traditional fermented foods from eastern Hainan.

    382 genomes assembled; 348 lactic acid bacteria isolated

    Food & Function 2022Food Res Int 2021
  2. 2 · ScreenProbiotic function

    Isolates screened in vitro for acid and bile tolerance and for production of the neurotransmitter GABA.

    L. plantarum HNU082 selected: GABA 216 mg/L

  3. 3 · ValidateIn vivo models

    Zebrafish larvae (n = 360) and two DSS-colitis mouse trials, prevention and treatment.

    Improved disease activity more than the drug sulfasalazine

    Food & Function 2022
  4. 4 · ExplainMechanism

    Metagenomics, colonic RNA-seq, short-chain fatty acids and MUC2 and ZO-1 staining.

    Microbial, chemical, mechanical and immune barriers restored

    Microbiology Spectrum 2022 · 273 citations
  5. 5 · TranslatePatent and industry

    Invention patent on the strain and its uses; I am the second-named inventor.

    Granted 2023; licensed to two companies (2023, 2024)

Diet, the gut–brain axis and fermentation Jiangnan University · 2023–present

Clinical Nutrition 2025

Diet, obesity and the gut–brain axis

In a four-month high-fat-diet mouse model, I integrated serum lipidomics, hippocampal proteomics, cytokine profiling, histology and behavioural testing. Lycopene reduced pro-inflammatory lipid species and preserved memory via taurine and glutathione pathways. A companion study on torularhodin is in revision.

Where I am heading areas I hope to contribute to as a postdoctoral researcher

Large population studies with molecular data can show how biology, environment and behaviour shape health across the life course. I want to turn such data into tools that find people at risk early, explain why, and point to what can be changed.
IBiomarkers of ageing and chronic disease

Develop and validate proteomic and other multi-omic markers of healthy ageing and of cardiometabolic, respiratory and other chronic diseases, in diverse populations, including Asian and multi-ethnic cohorts.

IIExposures, molecular pathways and health

Study how environmental, dietary and social exposures act through molecular pathways to shape disease risk, using longitudinal and causal-inference designs.

IIIFrom prediction to prevention

Combine population data, machine learning and experimental evidence to identify causal, modifiable targets that can inform precision prevention and intervention trials.
