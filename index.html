Research

An inflammation protein score for healthspan

Imperial College London · 2026–present

Can a blood test for inflammation tell us who will lose healthy years first? In 36,147 UK Biobank participants with Olink data, I built a score from 69 inflammation proteins for the first of seven major chronic diseases or death. The score adds information beyond age, including within narrow age groups. I then applied the same weights, unchanged, in EPIC (on the SomaScan platform), REACT and Airwave, each analysed inside a trusted research environment.

LASSO-Cox · cross-validation · bootstrap · calibration · time-dependent AUC · manuscript in preparation

Heat and cardiometabolic health

Imperial College London · 2026–present

As summers get hotter, what happens to blood pressure and to the kidney? I linked outdoor temperature to blood pressure in about 470,000 UK Biobank participants and used a case-crossover design to study hospital admissions after hot days. This work feeds a team proposal on heat and hypertension, where I run the preliminary cohort analyses.

Exposure linkage · case-crossover · conditional logistic regression · sensitivity analyses

Diet, obesity and the gut–brain axis

Jiangnan University · 2023–present

In a four-month high-fat-diet mouse model, I combined serum lipidomics, hippocampal proteomics, cytokines, histology and memory tests. Lycopene lowered pro-inflammatory lipids and protected memory through taurine and glutathione pathways. A second study on torularhodin and the gut–brain axis is in revision.

Clinical Nutrition 2025 · LC-MS/MS lipidomics · proteomics · behaviour

Gut microbiome and probiotics in colitis

Hainan University · 2020–2023

Two colitis mouse trials, one head-to-head against the drug sulfasalazine, showed that Lactobacillus plantarum HNU082 improved disease activity by restoring the gut barrier and reshaping the microbiome. I analysed shotgun metagenomics, 16S and colon RNA-seq on Linux servers.

Microbiology Spectrum 2022 · Food & Function 2022 · granted patent

Fermentation and evidence synthesis

Jiangnan University · 2023–present

A co-fermented sourdough that extends bread shelf life without chemical preservatives, and three critical reviews. The two most recent grade mechanistic claims on a predefined five-level evidence scale, with an evidence map.

Food Research International 2025 · J Food Sci 2026
